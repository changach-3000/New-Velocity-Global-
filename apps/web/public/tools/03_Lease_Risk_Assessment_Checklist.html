Risk Assessment Checklist

Check each item as confirmed. Incomplete items flag potential risk areas requiring attention before approval.

Customer Risk
Business case viability
Does the customer have a credible, documented business case for using this asset productively?
Cash flow adequacy
Can the asset generate or support sufficient cash flow to service the lease payment?
Credit history
Has the customer's credit history been reviewed — including previous defaults, restructures, or delinquencies?
Operating experience
Does the customer have proven experience operating this type of equipment?
Management quality
Is there competent management in place to oversee operations and asset use?
Personal guarantee
Has a personal or corporate guarantee been obtained and legally documented?
References verified
Have at least two trade or banking references been verified independently?
Asset Risk
Market value confirmed
Has the asset's fair market value been independently verified — not just the supplier's invoice?
Resale liquidity assessed
Is there an active secondary market for this asset if recovery is required?
Asset age and condition
Is the asset age and condition appropriate for the proposed lease term and residual value?
Residual value realistic
Is the residual value based on market evidence, not optimistic projections?
Specialisation risk
Is the asset generalist (widely usable) or highly specialised (hard to recover value from)?
Serial/chassis number recorded
Have the asset's unique identifiers been recorded for tracking and recovery purposes?
Legal & Documentation Risk
Ownership structure clear
Is legal ownership of the asset clearly established in the name of the lessor?
Lease agreement executed
Has the lease agreement been signed by authorised signatories on both sides?
Asset registered/insured
Is the asset registered in the lessor's name and fully insured for its replacement value?
Security interest filed
Has any applicable security interest or charge been registered with the relevant authority?
Default and recovery clauses
Does the lease agreement include clear, enforceable default and recovery provisions?
KYC/AML completed
Have Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering checks been completed and documented?
Usage & Operational Risk
Usage location confirmed
Has the primary usage location of the asset been confirmed and documented?
GPS/tracking installed
Is real-time asset tracking installed or required under the lease terms?
Maintenance schedule agreed
Is there a documented maintenance schedule and responsibility allocation?
Maintenance costs quantified
Have monthly maintenance costs been estimated and factored into the lessee's affordability assessment?
Insurance compliance monitored
Is there a process to verify the lessee maintains required insurance throughout the lease term?
Site visit conducted
Has a site visit or physical inspection of the business been completed before approval?
Portfolio & Concentration Risk
Sector concentration checked
Does this deal increase sector exposure beyond acceptable portfolio limits?
Geographic concentration checked
Is the geographic concentration of the portfolio within acceptable risk parameters?
Single-lessee exposure
Does this deal bring total exposure to this customer within approved limits?
Asset class diversification
Does adding this asset class maintain appropriate portfolio diversification?
