What Each Number Means — Plain Language
Implicit Rate (IRR) — The True Return
The implicit rate is not the interest rate you quote to the client. It is the actual internal rate of return on your investment — the rate that makes the net present value of all cashflows equal to zero. A deal quoted at "19% per annum" has an implicit rate of 19%. The gap between your implicit rate and your cost of funds is your gross margin.
—
Net Interest Spread — Your Margin Before Costs
The spread is what you earn above your cost of funds on every shilling deployed. A 5% spread on a KES 100M book = KES 5M per year in gross margin before operational costs and credit losses. Target spread for a Kenyan lessor: minimum 4%, strong at 6%+.
—
Rental Coverage Ratio — Can They Actually Pay?
The single most important credit metric in equipment leasing. It measures whether the lessee has enough free cashflow — after all their existing debt obligations — to comfortably cover your rental. Below 1.5× is a red flag. Above 2.0× is comfortable. The most common credit mistake is calculating coverage on raw EBITDA without deducting existing debt service.
