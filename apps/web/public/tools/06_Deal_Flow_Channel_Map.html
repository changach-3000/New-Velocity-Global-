The Core Principle
Mature leasing markets do not wait for customers to arrive. They embed financing into the equipment sales journey — at every point where equipment need is expressed. This map identifies your six primary deal flow channels, the action steps for each, and a tracking framework to build a consistent pipeline.
Pipeline Overview
🚛
Equipment Vendors & Dealers
🔴 High Priority
OEMs, dealerships, distributors
Your highest-volume channel. Dealers have customers who want equipment — the barrier is affordability. A structured referral programme turns every dealer into a leasing originator.
Action Steps
  1. Identify 5 anchor dealers in your target sector
  2. Propose referral commission (1.5–3% of financed value)
  3. Train dealer sales teams on how to present the lease option
  4. Provide co-branded marketing materials
  5. Set a monthly deal flow target per dealer

🏭
Industry Associations & Chambers
🟡 Medium Priority
Sector bodies, trade associations, chambers of commerce
Associations aggregate trust. A partnership with one association gives you access to all their members simultaneously — and positions you as the endorsed financing partner for the sector.
Action Steps
  1. Map the 3 most active associations in your target sector
  2. Propose a training/education partnership (VGL Podcast, masterclass)
  3. Offer members preferential leasing rates
  4. Speak at association events
  5. Negotiate endorsement or preferred partner status
Track this channel:
🔧
Service & Maintenance Centers
🟡 Medium Priority
Equipment service centres, authorised repair centres
Service centres know when equipment is ageing, failing, or becoming expensive to maintain. Every major service ticket is a potential conversation about equipment replacement finance.
Action Steps
  1. Partner with 2–3 major service centres in your sector
  2. Train service advisors to identify replacement finance opportunities
  3. Create a referral protocol: when maintenance cost exceeds X, refer to leasing
  4. Offer a commission on successful referrals
  5. Review service centre customer database for aged fleet opportunities
Track this channel:
🏦
Bank & SACCO SME Desks
🔴 High Priority
Commercial bank SME desks, SACCOs, MFIs
Many banks already serve SMEs who need equipment but cannot access conventional loans. The leasing opportunity is already inside the existing customer base — it just needs a product to serve it.
Action Steps
  1. Approach bank SME relationship managers with a co-financing proposal
  2. Offer to handle the equipment financing while bank handles working capital
  3. Propose a white-label leasing product for the bank's SME portfolio
  4. Target SACCOs serving logistics, agri, or construction members
  5. Provide training for bank staff on leasing referral identification
Track this channel:
🌾
Sector-Specific Campaigns
🔴 High Priority
Targeted outreach by sector and asset type
A focused leasing campaign targeting one sector converts better than a general message. Equipment needs in agriculture, logistics, healthcare, and construction are predictable and large-scale.
Action Steps
  1. Select 1 priority sector per quarter
  2. Identify the top 3 equipment types in that sector
  3. Build a targeted campaign (LinkedIn, WhatsApp, association events)
  4. Create sector-specific case studies and ROI examples
  5. Partner with a sector anchor (e.g. Isuzu for logistics, John Deere for agri)
Track this channel:
📱
Digital & Social Channels
🟡 Medium Priority
LinkedIn, WhatsApp Business, email campaigns
LinkedIn reaches CFOs, finance directors, and operations managers — your primary decision-makers. The VGL Podcast builds authority. WhatsApp drives direct conversations at scale.
Action Steps
  1. Publish 2 LinkedIn posts per week using VGL emotional hook framework
  2. Share LeaseTalk Africa podcast episodes targeting each sector
  3. Build a WhatsApp Business broadcast list of qualified prospects
  4. Run a monthly educational email to your mailing list
  5. Convert podcast listeners into masterclass and leasing enquiries
