Who This Tool Is For

This assessment is designed for: (a) Leasing companies applying to IFC, AfDB, PROPARCO, BII, or FMO for portfolio funding facilities, and (b) Banks seeking DFI co-financing or technical assistance for their lease portfolios.

DFIs will not fund a leasing operation that cannot demonstrate a credible ESG framework — regardless of how strong the financial metrics are. This tool structures the assessment so that gaps are identified and addressed before the formal DFI application is submitted.

E&S Management System (ESMS)
The structured framework DFIs require for ongoing ESG monitoring — not just at application
▾
What is an ESMS? An Environmental and Social Management System is the documented process by which a financial institution identifies, assesses, manages, and monitors the environmental and social risks in its portfolio. IFC requires an ESMS as a condition of investment for all financial intermediaries. Most Kenyan leasing companies do not have one — building it is the single most important DFI readiness action.
